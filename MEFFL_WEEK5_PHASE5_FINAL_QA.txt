COLLECTOR BACKEND FIX
The Phase 1 audit found that Yahoo renders trades with the action text "Traded to", while collector v1.3.8 only recognized the exact word "trade".
That caused the Oct 3 blockbuster to be omitted from structured transactions.

Collector updated to v1.3.9:
- recognizes both "trade" and "traded"
- extracts every player in a Yahoo "Traded to" row, including multi-player sides
- pairs same-timestamp trade rows so each destination team receives incoming and outgoing player lists
- keeps standard waiver/add/drop parsing unchanged

A sample parser test using the actual Yahoo trade layout correctly returned:
- Amon-Ra St. Brown on the Bogota side
- A.J. Brown, Chuba Hubbard and Bhayshul Tuten on the Great Communicator side
