THE RECEIPTS

Know your enemy

Three seasons is long enough for everyone to have a reputation, deserved or otherwise.

Harry

SVDBaller

22-20

The Points-For Torture Chamber
Harry has scored like a champion without ever getting to hold the trophy. In 2024 he actually outscored the 12-2 team and still finished third. He is the guy standing outside the club with the nicest outfit while everyone less qualified keeps getting waved inside.

Tommy

Wheat Hill Slow Blows

26-16

The Final Boss
The league's main character whether anybody likes it or not. Tommy went 6-8 in Year 1, won the title in Year 2, then went 12-2 and won it again in Year 3. At this point the rest of the league is not chasing a trophy, we're trying to stop a historical event. Beat him now or spend the next decade hearing about the three-peat.

Christopher

Rise of the Pleasure Machines

25-17

The Regular-Season Ferrari
Fast, expensive, beautiful, and occasionally bursts into flames exactly when everybody is watching. Chris went 9-5, then 12-2, then cratered to 4-10. When he is good, he looks unbeatable. When it goes bad, the smoke can be seen from space. Buffalo Bills energy in human form.

Tom

SFPAL Junior 49ers

6-8

The Expansion Team With Bad Luck
Tom entered the league in 2025, scored the fourth-most points, went 6-8, and finished eighth because fantasy football enjoys hazing the new guy. The first season was better than the record. Year 2 is where we find out whether that was bad luck or the beginning of a very specific kind of suffering.

Andrew

Bogota Booger Boys

20-22

The Reconstruction Worked
Two wins. Then seven. Then eleven. Andrew went from being the league's doormat to one of its top seeds in two years, which is the kind of turnaround that gets a coach a statue and gets the rest of us asking why the hell we let him figure it out.

Matty B.

Premature Ejleculators

13-29

Can a Man Escape His Own Ass?
Nine wins in 2023. Then 2-12. Then 2-12 again. Twenty-nine career losses and a fresh press conference on the résumé. The amazing part is that Matty B. is good at basically everything else in life. Fantasy football may simply be the universe's way of keeping him humble.

Owen

Jelq Me Jeantly

19-23

The Day Trader
56 moves in 2023. 72 in 2024. 68 in 2025. Owen does not have a roster, he has inventory. Every player is either a buy, sell, hold or guy who will be cut at 2:17 a.m. after one bad Thursday. The effort is unmatched. The stability is nonexistent. The entertainment is elite.

Matt F.

The Great Communicator

18-24

The Rabbit Has the Gun
Toilet Bowl in 2023. Eighth in 2024. Championship game in 2025. The Great Communicator has gone from league punchline to legitimate threat, and unfortunately he has become much harder to make fun of without checking the standings first. The rabbit indeed has the gun.

Patrick

The Breeder

22-20

Mr. January Without the Ring
Three seasons, three championship-bracket appearances. Pat can spend four months offering you his sixth-string running back for your best player, then somehow be standing in the playoffs like nothing happened. Big Government fantasy football: inefficient, confusing, and somehow always funded.

Danny

MCFISH

25-17

Draft It and Leave It Alone
Danny has 25 regular-season wins while treating the waiver wire like it charges by the click. He drafts, sets the lineup, and largely lets nature take its course. Sometimes nature gives you an 11-3 juggernaut. Sometimes it gives you the highest points-against total in the league and tells you to go fuck yourself.
