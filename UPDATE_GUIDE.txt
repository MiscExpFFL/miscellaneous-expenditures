MISCELLANEOUS EXPENDITURES — 2026 WEDNESDAY UPDATE GUIDE

NORMAL 2026 WORKFLOW
1. Wait until Wednesday waivers have processed.
2. Run the MEFFL Tampermonkey collector once.
3. Confirm Upcoming Week is the week about to be played. Collector v1.3.8 auto-suggests the upcoming week from the season calendar (and honors a Yahoo `week=` URL), but the number should still be checked before collecting.
4. Use the WEDNESDAY mode, then AUTO COLLECT LEAGUE.
5. Confirm every validation check is green.
6. Export the JSON and send it to ChatGPT.
7. ChatGPT performs one combined weekly site update and deploys it to the live GitHub Pages repo.

THE ONE WEDNESDAY COLLECTION MUST INCLUDE
- 10 standings teams with W-L, PF/PA, streak, FAAB and waiver priority where available
- all 5 final games from the completed week
- all 10 completed-week lineups and final starter-score reconciliation
- all 10 current post-waiver rosters
- all 5 upcoming-week matchups and Yahoo projections
- transactions / waiver moves / FAAB changes
- 15 QB, 25 RB, 25 WR, 15 TE, 10 K and 10 DEF available players

THE ONE WEDNESDAY SITE UPDATE MUST INCLUDE
PAST WEEK:
- five-game recap and final standings
- Game of the Week, high/low score, closest game, blowout and bad beat
- all-play and lineup-efficiency receipts
- prediction grading
- H2H, franchise history, Record Book and League Ledger updates
- past-week storylines and awards

WAIVERS / CURRENT STATE:
- waiver move and FAAB breakdown
- roster changes and remaining free-agent opportunities
- updated team needs and meaningful injuries/status changes

UPCOMING WEEK:
- full five-game preview
- locked ME winner and projected score for each matchup
- frozen Yahoo projection snapshot for comparison
- Game of the Week / Toilet Watch
- matchup-specific H2H/history context where useful
- lineup, injury, roster and playoff implications

MODEL / EDITORIAL REFRESH:
- Live Power Index / Power Rankings
- playoff, bye, title and Toilet odds
- projected playoff field and race tiers
- Franchise Stock Market
- weekly storylines and season timeline/pulse

SITE IMPORT
The public site continues to load:
season-2026.js -> weekly-import.js -> me-weekly-sync.js -> me-engine.js

The collector uses one Wednesday combined path. Completed games are historical facts; upcoming projections are preview data only.

SAFETY
- Only completed validated games may enter results/history/records/H2H.
- Upcoming Yahoo projections never become standings or historical scores.
- The Wednesday Yahoo projection snapshot is frozen beside the ME forecast for Predictions tracking.
- Every weekly write-up must cover all five completed games and all five upcoming matchups.
- The live-scoreboard UI stays disabled unless deliberately reactivated.
- The deployable weekly-snapshots archive is SANITIZED. Raw Yahoo page captures/sourceText and signed-in account text are not copied into the public site build.
- Keep the original downloaded collector JSON private.
- Never put Yahoo passwords, OAuth secrets, access tokens or refresh tokens in the public site.

DEPLOYMENT
Push the completed cumulative update to the existing live GitHub Pages repo. Do not change GoDaddy DNS, the GitHub Pages custom domain, or HTTPS settings.
