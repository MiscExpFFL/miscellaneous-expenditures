2026 DRAFT JUDGMENT DAY

So… who won the draft?

Tommy. By a nose. Makai Lemon at 11.01 and Jonah Coleman at 15.01 turn an already strong draft into the best blend of stars, keeper value and late upside. Harry still has the cleanest projected Week 1 roster, but the defending champ gets the draft-night belt.

THE VERDICT

Our 2026 draft rankings

Keeper value counts toward roster construction, but K-marked players are not treated like fresh draft selections. Yahoo graded the finished roster; we graded the roster, price paid and the manager likely to operate it.

DRAFT NIGHT HARDWARE

Draft awards

Some are compliments. Some are future evidence.

Draft winner

Tommy — Wheat Hill Slow Blows

Makai Lemon at 11.01 and Jonah Coleman at 15.01 break the tie: they are exactly the kind of upside picks a loaded roster should be making.

Best keeper

Christopher — Brock Bowers, Round 9

Elite tight-end production at a ninth-round slot is the kind of keeper value that changes the whole draft.

Best keeper pair

Harry — Chase Brown + Chris Olave

Two weekly starters at Round 6 and Round 9 costs. That is how you turn a good draft into the No. 1 roster.

Best fresh value

Tommy — Makai Lemon, Round 11

A rookie receiver with legitimate ceiling at 11.01 is the right kind of bench bet: the starting lineup does not need him immediately, but the payoff can be much bigger than the price.

Best final-round swing

Tommy — Jonah Coleman, Round 15

Instead of spending the last pick on a kicker, Tommy used it on another running-back contingency bet. That is how a loaded team should use Round 15.

Best late RB value

Matt F. — Tyjae Spears, Round 14

Getting a usable running back at 14.02 is exactly how a receiver-heavy roster should attack the endgame. Spears gives Matt F. another path to weekly RB production without sacrificing an earlier premium pick.

Best WR room

Tom — Puka + JSN + DeVonta

Tom leaned into the scoring change and built a target-volume cartel.

Best RB room

Andrew — Bijan + Breece

Two stars at the most fragile position, plus enough depth to avoid weekly panic.

Most Yahoo disrespect

Owen — Yahoo D / Our B+

The computer looked at Chase, Jeanty, Nico and a Round 10 Maye keeper and apparently got angry.

Most dangerous to himself

Matty B.

The roster is good enough to win. The question is whether Braddy’s Law can still find the one wrong lineup combination.
